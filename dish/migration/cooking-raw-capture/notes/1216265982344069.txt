Doenjang-jjigae

A compact stew of anchovy-dashima stock, young homemade doenjang, tofu, courgette, potato, onion, garlic and scallion.

WHY COOK IT
Build the central Korean stew block and test whether young doenjang has usable salinity, soybean depth, aroma and body.

CAN I COOK IT? No - awaiting independent verification.

## WHAT TO BUY
- Firm tofu: 300g
- Courgette: 180g
- Potato: 150g
- Dried anchovies and dashima, if absent

## CHECK BEFORE COOKING
- Use young homemade doenjang for the ferment test. Purchased doenjang is a recorded reference substitution.
- Do not use pork, clams, squid or mixed seafood in the baseline.

## QUANTITIES
Yield: 3 side-stew portions with rice.

Stock:
- Water: 900ml
- Dried anchovies: 12–15g
- Dashima: 8g
- Dried shiitake: 1, optional

Stew:
- Young homemade doenjang: begin with 45g; up to 60g after tasting
- Potato: 150g
- Onion: 100g
- Courgette: 180g
- Firm tofu: 300g
- Garlic: 10g
- Fresh green chilli or gochugaru: optional and restrained
- Scallion: 1

## HOW TO COOK IT
1. Soak dashima and anchovies in water for 20 minutes if time allows. Remove dashima before a hard boil; simmer anchovies for 10 minutes and strain.
2. Dissolve 45g doenjang into 750ml stock through a small sieve.
3. Add potato and onion; simmer until nearly tender.
4. Add courgette, tofu and garlic. Simmer for 5–7 minutes.
5. Taste. Add more doenjang only if both soybean depth and salinity are weak; loosen with stock or water if too intense.
6. Finish with scallion and optional chilli.

## WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
A concentrated compact stew with clear fermented-soy depth, tender vegetables and intact tofu. Brown broth flows between densely arranged components rather than diluting into soup.

Visual reference and free video — https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/doenjang-jjigae — Observe tofu and vegetables remaining individually visible and densely occupying the pot while enough brown broth flows between them. Ignore the source’s shrimp, larger doenjang dose and different stock sequence; it does not validate this young-doenjang test formula.

## WATCH OUT FOR
Do not season with soy before understanding the doenjang, boil dashima hard or dilute the stew into generic soup.

## STORAGE
Refrigerate up to 2 days; reheat gently.

### Research basis
Source-backed dish.
Construction update — Direct versions use two tablespoons doenjang for a meat-based stock. For a
vegetable/tofu pot with young homemade doenjang, start at 40g per 600ml anchovy-dashima stock and
reserve 10–15g for tasting. Add radish/potato first, then onion, tofu, zucchini, chilli and garlic;
finish with scallion. Research is sufficient; paste salinity is the intended calibration.
Sources: <https://www.koreanbapsang.com/doenjang-jjigae-korean-soy-bean-paste/>

The task makes about 900ml stock but dissolves the doenjang into only 750ml of it, deliberately
holding back roughly 150ml rather than scaling the seasoning to the full yield. Marco confirmed this
concentrated approach is preferred for the first attempt. A second source, Drive Me Hungry's
doenjang-jjigae (1 cup stock : 1 tablespoon doenjang), supports a much more concentrated ratio than a
full-yield dilution would give, consistent with jjigae's typical dense-stew character.
