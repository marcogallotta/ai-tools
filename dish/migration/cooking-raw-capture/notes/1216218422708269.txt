Thịt kho-style halal

Beef shank pressure-braised with nước màu, fish sauce, soy sauce and coconut water, then reduced uncovered with hard-boiled eggs. This is an explicit beef adaptation of a pork-centred Vietnamese dish.

WHY COOK IT
Test caramel control, fish-sauce braising, beef texture and egg seasoning in an Instant Pot-to-open-reduction workflow.

CAN I COOK IT? No - awaiting independent verification.

## CHECK BEFORE COOKING
- Buy 900g boneless edible beef shank or geretto; beef cheek is the second choice and chuck the third.
- Hard-boil and peel 3 eggs separately; eggs enter only after pressure release.
- This counts as the week's red-meat dish.

## QUANTITIES
Portions: 3 with rice.
- Beef shank: 900g boneless edible weight
- Fish sauce: 37ml
- Soy sauce: 68ml [approx]
- White sugar for caramel: 28g [approx]
- Water to stop caramel: 30ml
- Coconut water: 175–200ml
- Additional Instant Pot water: 30–80ml initially
- Shallot: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Onion: 1 medium
- Black pepper: about 3/4 tsp
- Eggs: 3, hard-boiled and peeled

## HOW TO COOK IT
1. Cook sugar to dark amber. Remove from direct heat and carefully stop with 30ml water; dissolve gently.
2. Brown beef in batches, protecting the caramel from scorching.
3. Add shallot, garlic and onion briefly.
4. Add fish sauce, soy sauce, coconut water and only 30–80ml extra water. Meat need not be submerged.
5. Pressure-cook for 45 minutes; natural release 15–20 minutes, then vent carefully.
6. If the beef remains tight, pressure-cook another 8–10 minutes.
7. Transfer to sauté mode or a wide pan. Add eggs and reduce uncovered for 15–20 minutes, turning gently.
8. Stop when the beef is fork-tender and the sauce glossy and clinging.

## WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
Beef is tender without shredding, caramel is dark but not burnt, and sauce is savoury-sweet rather than candy-like. Large meat pieces and whole eggs remain distinct in a mahogany, glossy, stew-like liquid rather than a sticky lacquer.

Visual analogue — https://www.seriouseats.com/vietnamese-food-101-a-guide-to-vietnamese-food-8422405 — Observe the thịt-kho-trứng image for mahogany colour, egg integrity and stew-like liquid body. Ignore pork belly fat layers and stovetop tenderness cues; the image does not validate beef or pressure-cooker behaviour.

## WATCH OUT FOR
Beef changes the fat, collagen and sweetness of the pork original. Do not pressure-cook eggs, use stovetop liquid volumes, leave caramel unattended or skip final reduction.

## STORAGE
Refrigerate and eat within a few days. Reheat gently with a little water if necessary. No freezer baseline.
