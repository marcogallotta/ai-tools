Chicken karahi
Chicken karahi maintenance — green chilli and layered ginger

Chicken cooked in a concentrated tomato, chilli and ginger masala without onion or yogurt.

WHY COOK IT
Keep the already-successful karahi skill fresh and preserve the proper green-chilli effect.

CAN I COOK IT? Yes — provided proper South Asian green chillies are available.

## QUANTITIES
- Bone-in chicken thighs: 900g
- Tomatoes or pelati: 600g
- Neutral oil: 60ml
- Garlic: 20g
- Fresh ginger: 35g, split cooked and fresh
- South Asian green chillies: 25–35g, split
- Cumin seed: 1 tsp
- Coriander seed: 2 tsp
- Black pepper: 1 tsp
- Kashmiri chilli: optional 1 tsp
- Fine salt: begin with 9g
- Lemon juice: optional 0–15ml after tasting

## HOW TO COOK IT
1. Bloom cumin and brown chicken in batches if needed.
2. Add garlic, half the ginger and half the green chilli.
3. Add tomato, coriander, pepper, optional chilli and salt.
4. Cook uncovered until tomato collapses and masala clings with oil at the edges.
5. Add small amounts of hot water only if chicken needs more time.
6. Finish with remaining ginger and chilli; add lemon only if needed.

## WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
Chicken remains intact and tender. Tomato collapses into a tight glossy masala with only small oil beads and no watery pool. Fresh ginger and green chilli remain distinct; the dish does not resemble onion- or yogurt-based gravy.

Visual sourcing gap — generic kadai-chicken media commonly includes onion, yogurt or bell pepper at the same sauce-body boundary. Photograph tomato collapse and the finished ginger-and-chilli masala during the maintenance cook.

## WATCH OUT FOR
Do not crowd the pan, add a generic onion base or add lemon automatically.
