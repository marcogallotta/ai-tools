Bibimbap

Rice topped with separately cooked and seasoned vegetables, egg, kimchi if appropriate and a staged gochujang sauce.

WHY COOK IT
Build Korean rice-table composition after simpler namul and ferment blocks have been assessed.

CAN I COOK IT? No - awaiting independent verification.

## WHAT TO BUY
- Short- or medium-grain rice
- Soybean sprouts
- Spinach
- Courgette
- Carrot
- Mushrooms
- Eggs

## CHECK BEFORE COOKING
- Use young homemade gochujang only if ready; otherwise use a reference halal-suitable gochujang and record the substitution.
- Keep beef out of the first version unless deliberately requested.

## QUANTITIES
Yield: 3 substantial bowls. With no beef, tofu or other named protein, committed protein is roughly
12-15g/bowl (egg plus vegetables), well under the ~50g target. Accepted as a deliberate consequence
of the no-beef decision below, not an oversight; if a fuller-protein version is wanted later, add a
stated protein component rather than inflating egg count alone.
- Short- or medium-grain rice: 300g dry
- Soybean sprouts: 300g
- Spinach: 250g
- Courgette: 250g
- Carrot: 180g
- Dried shiitake: 25g dry, rehydrated, or 200g fresh mushrooms
- Eggs: 3
- Neutral oil, sesame oil, garlic, scallion, salt, soy and sesame seeds as needed

Gochujang sauce:
- Young homemade or reference gochujang: 45g
- Sesame oil: 11ml
- White sugar: 6g
- Water: 11ml
- Garlic: 5g, optional

## HOW TO COOK IT
1. Cook rice and keep it hot.
2. Prepare soybean sprouts as kongnamul-muchim.
3. Blanch spinach briefly, squeeze dry and season lightly.
4. Stir-fry courgette until just tender; remove.
5. Stir-fry carrot until tender-crisp; remove.
6. Cook mushrooms with a small amount of soy and garlic until glossy and no longer wet.
7. Mix the sauce and fry eggs fresh.
8. Arrange components separately over rice. Add sauce gradually and mix at the table.

## WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
Each vegetable remains separately legible in distinct bands over hot rice. Sauce binds the mixed bowl without erasing the component identities, and young gochujang, if used, contributes ferment depth without raw or harsh notes.

Visual reference and free video — https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/bibimbap — Observe each vegetable cooked and held separately, then arranged in distinct bands over rice before mixing. This validates component separation and final assembly. Ignore the source's beef, raw-yolk option, expanded vegetable set and dolsot branch.

## WATCH OUT FOR
Do not cook all vegetables together, over-season every component or use bibimbap as the first simultaneous test of every homemade ferment.

## STORAGE
Store components separately up to 2 days. Cook rice and eggs fresh when possible.
