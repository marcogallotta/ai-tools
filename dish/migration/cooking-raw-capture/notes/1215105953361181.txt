Brik — Tunisian tuna-and-egg malsouka parcel

A thin malsouka sheet folded around tuna, onion, parsley, capers, harissa and a whole egg, then shallow-fried quickly so the wrapper crisps before the yolk hardens.

WHY COOK IT
Continue calibration of proper-wrapper handling, leakage control, oil heat and yolk timing in a dish already attempted.

CAN I COOK IT? Yes.

## WHAT TO BUY
- Eggs: one per brik
- Tuna: about 50g drained per brik
- Lemon for serving

## CHECK BEFORE COOKING
- Proper warka/malsouka is already on hand.
- Cook one brik at a time and eat it immediately.
- Use a small safe pan, wide spatula and lid or splatter protection nearby.

## QUANTITIES
Per brik:
- Malsouka/warka wrapper: 1
- Egg: 1
- Drained tuna: about 45–50g
- Onion: about 25g, finely chopped and softened
- Parsley: 6–7g
- Capers: about 4–5g
- Harissa: 1.5–3g
- Black pepper
- Frying oil: enough for about 2cm depth

## HOW TO COOK IT
1. Soften the onion in a very small amount of oil; cool. Mix with tuna, parsley, capers, harissa and pepper.
2. Heat about 2cm oil over medium heat. A numeric 175–180°C target is an approximate operating range, not a sourced requirement.
3. Lay the wrapper over a shallow plate. Place filling off-centre, make a clear well and crack in the egg.
4. Fold immediately into a rectangle, triangle or half-moon and slide seam-first into the oil.
5. Fry one at a time, turning once, until both faces are blistered and golden. Begin assessing at about 2 minutes total; wrapper colour and yolk preference control the endpoint.
6. Drain briefly and serve immediately with lemon.

## WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
The wrapper seals without egg leakage and becomes thin, blistered and crisp rather than greasy. Filling is hot and the yolk is soft-to-jammy, not cold-raw or hard-boiled.

Visual/process reference — https://cultureatz.com/tunisian-brick/ — Observe the tuna-onion-caper-parsley-harissa filling, egg well, immediate folding and shallow frying. Ignore its spring-roll/filo fallback because proper malsouka is available.

## WATCH OUT FOR
A leaking egg can spit violently. Do not overfill, pre-assemble, chase an exact clock over wrapper colour or fry several for holding.

## STORAGE
None after assembly. Keep wrappers covered and filling chilled; crack the egg only immediately before frying.
