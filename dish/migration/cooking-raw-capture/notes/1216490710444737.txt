Korma repair — yogurt and nut emulsion without over-reduction

A chicken-korma repair focused on keeping the yogurt, nut and fat sauce smooth, loose and integrated.

WHY COOK IT
Repair the known emulsion and over-reduction failure rather than repeat it under a new flavour formula.

CAN I COOK IT? Yes.

## WHAT TO BUY
- Bone-in chicken: 800–900g
- Onion: 250g
- Full-fat plain yogurt: 350g
- Raw cashews or blanched almonds: 30g total

## CHECK BEFORE COOKING
- Use raw rather than roasted nuts to reduce variables.
- Have 250ml hot water ready before yogurt enters; water is an emulsion phase, not an emergency rescue.
- Do not chase oil separation as a doneness signal.

## QUANTITIES
Portions: 3.
- Bone-in chicken: 800–900g
- Onion: 250g
- Full-fat yogurt: 350g
- Cashews or almonds: 30g, blended very smooth with 80–120ml water
- Ghee or neutral oil: 45ml
- Garlic: 15g
- Ginger: 15g
- Green cardamom: 4
- Cloves: 4
- Cassia: 3cm
- Bay leaf: 1
- Coriander seed: 2 tsp, ground fresh
- Cumin seed: 1 tsp, ground fresh
- Kashmiri chilli: optional 1 tsp
- Fine salt: begin with 8g
- Hot water: 150–250ml available
- Own garam masala: optional 1/2 tsp late

## HOW TO COOK IT
1. Fry onions gently until golden. Blend part or all with the nuts and enough water for a completely smooth loose paste.
2. Brown chicken lightly; do not develop a hard crust.
3. Add whole spices, garlic and ginger, then ground spices with a splash of water.
4. Return chicken with 150ml hot water and partly cook it.
5. Add the loose onion-nut paste. Keep the sauce flowing; add hot water before it tightens.
6. Whisk yogurt smooth. Temper it with several spoonfuls of hot sauce, lower the heat and add gradually while stirring.
7. Simmer very gently until chicken is tender, adding hot water as necessary.
8. Stop when sauce falls from a spoon in a broad ribbon and settles around the meat. Rest before final judgment.

## WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
Chicken is tender in a smooth pale-to-golden sauce that coats while still flowing. It is neither grainy nor split, does not stand as nut paste and shows no large separated oil layer.

Visual/process reference — https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/chicken-korma-recipe/ — Observe golden onion and nuts blended with yogurt, a smooth base and hot water maintaining a flowing gravy. Ignore its marinade, optional tomato and exact formula.

## WATCH OUT FOR
Do not boil hard after adding yogurt, reduce until oil separates, add dry nut paste or wait until the sauce has seized before adding water.

## STORAGE
Refrigerate up to 3 days. Reheat gently with water; do not boil.
