Chicken jalfrezi
Chicken jalfrezi — dry high-heat baseline

Chicken and crisp vegetables cooked separately, then combined in a light tomato-chilli coating.

WHY COOK IT
Establish a Subcontinent dry high-heat system distinct from bhuna, korma and dal.

CAN I COOK IT? Yes.

## QUANTITIES
Portions: 3.
- Chicken breast: 600g, 3cm pieces
- Bell peppers: 350g
- Onion: 250g, petals
- Tomatoes: 300g
- Garlic: 15g
- Ginger: 20g, half held for finish
- Fresh green chillies: 15–25g
- Neutral oil: 45ml
- Cumin seed: 1 tsp
- Coriander seed: 2 tsp
- Turmeric: 1/2 tsp
- Kashmiri chilli: 1 tsp
- Garam masala: 1/2 tsp
- Fine salt: begin with 8g
- Lemon juice: optional 10ml

## HOW TO COOK IT
1. Cook chicken in uncrowded high-heat batches until browned and just cooked; remove.
2. Stir-fry peppers and onion separately until blistered but crisp; remove.
3. Bloom cumin, then add garlic, half the ginger and chilli.
4. Add tomato and ground spices; reduce rapidly to a light coating sauce.
5. Return chicken and vegetables and toss only until glossy and hot.
6. Finish with ginger julienne, the garam masala, and optional lemon.

## WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
Chicken remains browned and juicy. Pepper and onion retain distinct shapes, blistered edges and crisp-tender structure. Tomato forms a light glossy coating with no pooled liquid or thick bhuna-like paste.

Visual reference — https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/chicken-jalfrezi/ — Observe peppers and onions stir-fried separately, removed and returned for a short final toss; finished vegetables stay distinct in a semi-dry coating. Ignore pre-cooked chicken, sauces, kasuri methi, fennel and exact tomato ratio.

## WATCH OUT FOR
Do not crowd the pan, turn tomato into bhuna or overcook vegetables during recombination.
